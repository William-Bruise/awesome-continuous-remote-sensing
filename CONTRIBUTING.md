# Contributing to Awesome Continuous Remote Sensing

Suggest **published papers** that directly address Earth/remote sensing, geophysical fields, or geospatial sensing and meet the white-paper themes in [CURATION.md](CURATION.md).

For every proposal, supply:

1. Exact article title, final venue and publication year, DOI or official publisher/proceedings link.
2. **One primary theme**: representation, harmonization, or physical inference. Add a *secondary theme* only for a demonstrable overlap.
3. Explicit continuous coordinates/variables (e.g., `(x,y,z,λ,t,view)`) and an evidence-backed explanation of **what is continuous**.
4. A task-specific account of **why the representation matters** and which actual sensor modalities or physical fields are involved.
5. Journal venue's **JCR Q1 category/year** or confirmation that this is a **Full/Regular main-track** paper at CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, NeurIPS, ICLR, ICML, AAAI, ACM MM, SIGGRAPH or SIGGRAPH Asia.
6. The **official code repository** URL, if released and verified. Leave blank otherwise.

Do **not** submit: arXiv-only works, workshops, Findings, short papers, generic foundation algorithms with no remote-sensing/geophysical task, normal discrete-only multimodal CNNs, or articles that pass only a CAS 1/2 or SCImago Q1 gate but are not JCR Q1.

See [data/papers.csv](data/papers.csv) and [data/paper_level_audit.csv](data/paper_level_audit.csv). Journal quartiles and links should be checked against the applicable publication/JCR edition; flag uncertainty rather than assuming acceptance.
